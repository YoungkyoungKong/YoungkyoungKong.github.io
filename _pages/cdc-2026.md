*The 65th IEEE Conference on Decision and Control*, 2026. Extended version.

[[Extended version (PDF)]](/files/cdc2026_extended_ver.pdf)

## Abstract

We investigate online control of nonlinear systems with unknown, bounded, and potentially adversarial disturbances without stochastic assumptions. In this setting, minimizing the regret is essential for achieving nearly optimal control performance. While online non-stochastic control has been proposed to achieve robustness while minimizing regret for linear systems, extensions to nonlinear dynamics remain limited. We extend this discussion to nonlinear systems by leveraging Koopman operator theory. We show that a nonlinear system with disturbances can be exactly lifted to a linear parameter-varying system with lifted disturbances, which we further approximate by a linear time-invariant model with a bounded residual. For this lifted system, we design a controller and update the parameters of the proposed residual-action policy using online convex optimization with memory. We theoretically and numerically show that our algorithm achieves a sublinear regret bound and yields bounded state and input trajectories of the closed-loop system.
