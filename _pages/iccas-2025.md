## Abstract

This paper proposes data collection conditions for data-driven system identification of nonlinear systems that admit a Koopman linear embedding. We integrate an identifiability condition from behavioral systems theory into a Koopman-based system identification framework and derive a rank condition on a Hankel matrix constructed from collected data. The resulting condition guarantees identifiability and establishes a theoretical lower bound on the number of data points required for identifying nonlinear systems with linear embedding.
