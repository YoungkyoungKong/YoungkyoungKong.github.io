## Research Interests

My research focuses on online adaptation and learning for robotic systems operating in changing environments. I aim to develop methods that enable deployed policies to adapt to environmental changes and disturbances in real time. My goal is to achieve this without retraining or redesigning policies from scratch, with an emphasis on performance, robustness, and safety guarantees.
